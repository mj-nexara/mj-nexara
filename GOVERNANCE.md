# 🏛️ Governance Policy

## Mission

Stewardship, not ownership—this repository exists for the public good, not for individual or private gain.  
All governance is participatory, open, and accountable.

---

## Principles

1. **No Individual Ownership:**  
   No one, including the founder, may claim private ownership or exclusive authority.
2. **Transparency:**  
   All proposals, discussions, and decisions are public and documented.
3. **Consensus-Oriented:**  
   Major changes require broad consensus among active stewards and contributors.
4. **Auditability:**  
   All decisions are logged in [AUDIT.md](./AUDIT.md).
5. **Succession & Continuity:**  
   If a lead steward departs, new stewards are selected transparently and with community input.

---

## Decision-Making Process

1. **Proposal Submission:**  
   Anyone may propose a change via GitHub Issue or Pull Request.
2. **Discussion:**  
   Open discussion for at least 72 hours (or more for major changes).
3. **Consensus & Voting:**  
   Major changes require at least 2 approving maintainers and no major objections.
4. **Documentation:**  
   Every accepted/rejected proposal is logged in [AUDIT.md](./AUDIT.md).

---

## Stewardship

All maintainers and contributors must act as stewards—servants of the public good, not owners of the code.

---
