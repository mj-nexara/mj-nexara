**Irrevocable Dedication to Humanity & Future Generations**

## Core Principles

1. **No Private Ownership:**  
   All code, knowledge, and resources are public commons; private claims are forbidden.

2. **No Personal Financial Gain:**  
   No one may use any part of this system for private enrichment or personal financial transactions.

3. **Transparency & Auditability:**  
   Every action and decision must be documented and remain open for public audit at all times.

4. **Stewardship & Accountability:**  
   All contributors and maintainers are stewards, not owners; full accountability and traceability are mandatory.

5. **Irrevocable Dedication:**  
   These principles are permanent and cannot be revoked or bypassed by anyone, including the founder or future stewards.
